Critterwave Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Critterwave Android application (the “App”), developed by Bunhouse Labs. It does not govern the independent practices of Google Play, Android, or other services you use.

Information processed on your device

The App stores your chosen critter name, character selections, game progress, records, and preferences, including theme, sound, and motion settings. It uses this information to operate the game, resume play, and remember your settings. Use a nickname for your critter if you do not wish to enter your real name.

The App does not transmit this information to Bunhouse Labs or to third parties. It does not require an account, display advertising, or include analytics or advertising trackers. Bunhouse Labs does not sell or share App data.

Storage and security

The App stores its data in application storage on your device. Access to that storage is governed by Android’s application isolation and your device’s security settings. Depending on your device and backup settings, Android may back up or transfer App data independently of the App. Bunhouse Labs does not operate or control those backup services.

Retention and deletion

App data remains in application storage until it is replaced or removed. The in-game Clear Data command resets saved progress and records but retains some preferences. To remove all data in the App’s local storage, clear the App’s storage in Android settings or uninstall it. Any copies retained by Android backup services are managed through your device or backup account settings.

Google Play

Google may process information independently when you obtain or update the App through Google Play or use related platform services. Google’s privacy policy governs that processing.

Privacy inquiries and support

If you contact Bunhouse Labs, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to and administer your inquiry. We retain that correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to address the matter or as required by law. To ask about this policy or request deletion of correspondence, contact bunhouselabs@gmail.com.
